# Initial Concept
MindToosa

## 1. Product Focus
MindToosa is a comprehensive productivity application that serves as:
- A personal daily planner and focus timer.
- A comprehensive project management and goal-tracking system.

## 2. Core Features & User Flows
The primary features of the platform include:
- Daily task scheduling and Pomodoro-style focus sessions.
- Weekly overviews and long-term goal setting.
- Project dashboards with Kanban boards or task lists.

## 3. Target Audience
The platform is designed to assist:
- Professionals and freelancers managing independent projects and billing.
- Students and academics needing structured study plans.
- Individuals seeking personal growth, habit building, and general organization.
- Project management teams.

## 4. Problems Solved
MindToosa aims to solve the following challenges for its users:
- Difficulty focusing and managing time effectively throughout the day.
- Losing track of long-term goals amidst daily tasks.
- Lack of organization and context-switching for multiple projects.
- Sub-tasking complex projects/tasks.

## 5. Success Metrics
The primary indicators of success for a user include:
- Number of Pomodoro sessions completed or focused hours logged.
- Percentage of planned daily tasks successfully finished.
- Consistent progress towards and completion of long-term goals.
- **Critical Feature:** The application's AI must be able to break down complex tasks into smaller, easier-to-manage sub-tasks.
