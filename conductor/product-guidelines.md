# Product Guidelines

## 1. Tone and Voice

- **Professional & Focus-Oriented:** The language should be concise, direct, and non-intrusive. It should gently guide the user toward their next action without unnecessary fluff.
- **Empathetic encouragement:** Provide subtle positive reinforcement upon completing tasks or reaching milestones, to keep motivation high.

## 2. Visual Identity

- **Minimalist & Clean:** The interface should minimize cognitive load. Use ample whitespace, clear typography, and a restrained color palette that adheres to a "dark mode" or visually resting aesthetic by default.
- **Distraction-Free:** Key interfaces like the daily planner and Pomodoro timer must strip away extraneous elements, focusing entirely on the task at hand.

## 3. User Experience (UX) Principles

- **Frictionless Entry:** Adding a task, starting a timer, or checking off an item should require the absolute minimum number of clicks.
- **AI as a Facilitator:** AI features (like breaking down complex tasks) should be suggested contextually rather than forced. They should feel like a helpful assistant that appears exactly when you feel overwhelmed.
- **Seamless Context Switching:** Moving between the daily view (micro) and the project/weekly view (macro) should feel fluid, maintaining the user's context.

## 4. Technical Constraints & Performance

- **Speed is a Feature:** The application must remain highly responsive. Interactions should feel immediate to maintain the user's flow state.
